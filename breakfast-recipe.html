Morning Oatmeal from the Shire

Breakfast • 15 min • Easy • Serves 4 to 6

Unlike improvised gruel made from oats and water, the kind of breakfast a weary traveler might make while undertaking a long journey, such as the Hobbits did, oatmeal made at home with full access to milk, honey, fruits, jellies, and nuts can be a sumptuous feast fit for Hobbits, women and men alike.

Instructions

  1. Put the milk, water, vanilla extract, cinnamon, and salt in a large saucepan over medium heat and bring slowly to a boil. Stir in the oats, then reduce the heat and simmer gently, stirring occasionally, for 8 to 10 minutes until creamy and tender.
  2. Spoon the oatmeal into bowls and serve with the topping of your choice.

Ingredients:

  • 1¾ pints dairy or dairy-free milk
  • 2 cups, plus 2 tablespoons water
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Pinch of ground cinnamon
  • Pinch of salt
  • 2 cups rolled oats

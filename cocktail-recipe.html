Sugar Plum Fairy Cocktail

Drinks • 25 min • Easy • Serves 4 to 6

Unlike ordinary sweet drinks enjoyed on quiet evenings, the Sugar Plum Fairy Cocktail is a magical creation inspired by enchanted forests and fairy gardens. Crafted with delicate plum flavors, floral notes, sparkling touches, and a hint of sweetness, this whimsical potion transforms a simple sip into a charming celebration fit for fairies, woodland creatures, and dreamers alike.

Instructions

  1. De-seed a fresh pomegranate.
  2. Boil 1 cup water and 1 cup sugar together in a pot along with about 1/2 cup pomegranate seeds.
  3. Boil until the sugar is fully dissolved.
  4. Turn off the heat and let cool.
  5. Strain out the seeds and store your pomegranate syrup in the fridge.
  6. Pour your coating sugar onto a plate.
  7. Rub the rim of your cocktail glasses with lemon juice or water (anything to help the sugar stick!) and press the rim into the sugar to line the rim of your glass.
  8. Mix the gin, simple syrup, and egg white in a cocktail shaker, then shake well.
  9. Add in a few ice cubes to the shaker and shake again.
  10. Pour the mixture into your glasses, leaving a few inches at the top for the soda.
  11. Top with the Sugar Plum Sparkling Beverage and serve!

Ingredients:

  • Pink sugar to coat the rim of your glass
  • 1.5 oz Empress Gin
  • 1 egg white
  • 1.5 oz pomegranate simple syrup
  • Sugar Plum Sparkling Beverage to top
  • Ice
